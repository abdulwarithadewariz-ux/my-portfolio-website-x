02 / about

A bit about me.

I'm based in Kaduna, I study Computer Science at Al-Hikmah University in Ilorin, and I'm a software developer who learns by building real things.

I started with Python and kept going. Most of what I know came from turning an idea into an application, hitting a problem I didn't expect, and staying with it until the thing actually worked. That loop — build, break, debug, ship — is still how I learn best.

Today I build full-stack products: a football platform that bridges three data providers and stays honest about missing data (Machora), an installable Islamic lifestyle app with payments, auth and offline support (Deenly), and an AI study studio (Dopamind). I care about the whole path — modelling the data, designing the interface, integrating APIs, and deploying something people can actually open.

I'm looking for internships, remote roles and collaborations where I can work on real product problems alongside engineers who know more than I do. I'm equally interested in full-stack development and AI-powered applications, and I'm always learning the next thing that a project demands.
